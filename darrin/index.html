Outline

What You Want

  • Get back running
  • Back to where you were middle of last year. A competent level of fitness, HIIT, turf games
  • Get back the strength you've noticed you've lost
  • Back on the bike, which you've been told to stay off

What's Actually Going On

  • Stress fracture mid-April, confirmed on the MRI. You thought it was shin splints at the time, so you carried on walking on it
  • Bupa gave you crutches and said don't touch the floor. You couldn't do that, you were building the studio gym
  • You reckon it's 90 to 95% healed. No limp, stairs are fine, no pain day to day
  • 10,000 steps might leave a really dull ache the next morning. A 1 out of 10

The Problem…

  • Two months with no lower body work at all. Back in June you were deadlifting 130 to 140kg and squatting fine
  • No conditioning either. The bike was the one thing you could do and that got taken off you as well

The Fix

What I Would Do Instead

  • Start loading it. Isometrics first, even just holding certain positions on the floor, then literally build up from there and stay cautious with any pain.
  • Rehab equals training. You've not loaded your legs in two months, so we get everything strong alongside the shin. Calf raises, quads, hamstrings, glutes.
  • Get the conditioning back in. The bike goes straight back on. That way, by the time you're running again, you're not gassing out.
  • Back to running around the 12 week mark. That's realistic from where you are now, built up gradually rather than jumping straight back into 10k.

Structure

Upper Body × 2 Per Week

  • The chest, shoulders and back work you're already doing
  • I programme it too, so the whole week is planned, not just the shin

Daily Mobility (5 Mins)

  • Five minutes a day, that's it

Off Feet Conditioning

  • The bike comes back in
  • Builds your fitness back while the shin is still being loaded gradually

Progression

How The 12 Weeks Work

Four phases. You don't move into the next one because a certain number of weeks have gone by. You move when you hit the criteria.

Strength

Hitting the numbers for that phase.

Confidence

Trusting the leg under load.

Pain

The big one. Minimal to no pain and we sail through. Loads of pain and we back off.
